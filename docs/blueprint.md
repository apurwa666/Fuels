# **App Name**: FuelMails Landing

## Core Features:

- Navbar: Top navigation bar including links to 'About Us,' 'Case Studies,' 'Designs,' and 'Blog'.
- Email Template Carousel: Carousel featuring email design templates with smooth transitions and interactive hover effects.
- Animated Statistics: Animated stat counters for 'Total Revenue Generated', 'Emails Sent', and 'Our Clients'.
- Case Studies and Designs: Grid layout for showcasing featured email campaign case studies with interactive hover animations on a new 'Design' page.
- Blog Previews: A 'Read Our Blog' section featuring previews of recent blog posts with clickable links.
- Contact and Scheduling: Footer with 'Contact Us' section linked to a calendar popup for scheduling calls.
- Blog SEO Generator: AI tool generates summary description and SEO-friendly name and descriptions from blog posts.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Sunset Orange (#FF8C42) to represent energy and forward momentum, and evokes positive emotion.
- Background color: Light Orange (#FFF2E6), a desaturated shade of the primary color.
- Accent color: Deep Violet (#8042FF), providing a vibrant contrast to the primary, while still being near it in hue.
- Body and headline font: 'Poppins' (sans-serif) for a modern and clean look.
- Minimal, custom icons to accompany stat counters.
- Responsive grid layouts optimized for both mobile and desktop.
- Subtle animations and micro-interactions on hover and scroll.
